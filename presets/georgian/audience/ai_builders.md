# AI Builders

Daily practitioners inside tech startups — engineers, PMs, and product builders who use AI hands-on to ship better products and services, and who also build AI-powered features themselves.

- Write for someone who is already in the work, not evaluating whether to start.
- Prioritize actionable takeaways: tools, patterns, and decisions they can apply immediately.
- Cover both sides of the practitioner's world: using AI as a force-multiplier in development, and designing AI into the product itself.
- Be specific and honest about tradeoffs — what breaks at scale, what requires judgment, where the hype diverges from reality.
- Skip executive framing and market commentary. They care about craft, not positioning.
